## The AI Advisor can now take action

![Advisor Actions](/images/changelog/2026-07/advisor-takes-action.jpg)

You can now ask the Foliofox AI Advisor to:

- Create a new position in your portfolio
- Add buy, sell, deposit, withdrawal, and other portfolio records
- Record transactions using the details from your conversation

For example, you can tell it that you bought five shares of an ETF, deposited cash into an account or opened a new position.

Before making any change, Foliofox shows you exactly what will be added and asks for your confirmation. Nothing is changed without your approval.

## Ask Foliofox about Foliofox

![Ask Foliofox about Foliofox](/images/changelog/2026-07/ask-foliofox-about-foliofox.jpg)

The AI Advisor can now also answer questions about the product itself.

You can ask how broker imports work, what different record types mean, how cost basis is calculated, how to structure a CSV file or how to use a particular Foliofox feature.

If you have any question about Foliofox, try ask the advisor directly.

## Directa and Scalable Capital imports

Broker Import now supports two additional brokers:

- Directa
- Scalable Capital

You can export your transaction history from your broker and upload it to Foliofox. Foliofox can create missing positions, import your buy and sell history, detect duplicates and rebuild your portfolio timeline automatically.

We also improved the review process for instruments that cannot be matched automatically, partial broker exports and securities that are not available through our market-data provider.

## 🇮🇩 Indonesian Rupiah support

Foliofox now supports the Indonesian rupiah, or IDR, making it possible to track IDR-denominated assets and use IDR as a portfolio currency.
